The two laws behind the equation

Compound first. Then leverage.

Each stage of the programme runs on one law. The Foundation compounds small daily gains until they become who you are. The Multiplier puts that new capacity where a small effort can return an outsized result.

Law one · Foundation · Pivots 01–05
37X

The 37X compound

1.01365 = 37.8  ·  0.99365 = 0.03

Get 1% better at the things that matter, every day, and a year later you are operating at a different order of magnitude. Slip 1% a day and almost nothing is left. Vision, time, mindset, identity and self-worth are where the daily 1% is won or lost.

An illustration of compounding, not a forecast. Real growth is lumpier than this curve. The principle holds: small gains repeated daily beat occasional big pushes.

Asymmetric leverage

Capped downside · uncapped upside

Senior leaders don't grow by working harder. They grow by choosing moves where the cost is small and known and the payoff has no ceiling: one relationship, one visible point of view, one well-placed move with the right stakeholder, one moonshot run as a low-risk pilot first.

Questions
Before you apply.

    

      
How is this different from executive coaching?
Most coaching works on whatever feels urgent this week. The GROW$X Equation is a fixed sequence of ten pivots, each building on the last, aimed at one high-order vision. It covers the inner work (mindset, identity, self-worth) and the outer work (network, brand, power, impact, status) in the same programme, on your live role.

      
What are 37X and asymmetric leverage?
They are the two laws the programme runs on. 37X is the maths of compounding: 1% better a day for a year multiplies to about 37 times. It is a way of thinking about daily practice, not a promise of a number. Asymmetric leverage means choosing moves where the downside is small and known and the upside is uncapped. The Foundation pivots build your daily compound. The Multiplier pivots find your highest-leverage moves.

      
Which programme and delivery mode is right for me?
GROW$X Blueprint is the flagship for value creators: the full ten-pivot, ten-month programme, delivered one-to-one (GROW$XOne), with your leadership team (GROW$XTeam) or with non-competing peers (GROW$XTribe). Career Kaihagyo is for career builders reinventing their next move, one-to-one or peer-to-peer. The 37X Autonomy Protocol is a 12-month elite engagement, delivered one-to-one or through NextGen Bespoke and NextGen Tribe. Use Find your path above, or tell us in your Strategy Session and we will recommend one.

      
How do you measure results?
In your Strategy Session we agree the two or three numbers you are judged on, such as valuation multiple, portfolio returns, time to milestone or promotion velocity, and record your baseline. Each pivot moves a lead indicator we track monthly. At the end of the programme we compare your scoreboard with that baseline, so your progress is measured in your own numbers.

      
Is the method grounded in science?
It is built from 20 years of practice and grounded in behavioural science. The core tools, turning a vision into a goal with if-then plans and rehearsing new responses until they become automatic, are among the best-tested methods in the research on goals and habits.

      
Is the excavation work therapy?
No. It is performance work. We go into mindset, identity and self-worth because that is where high performers' ceilings usually sit, and we keep it tied to your vision and your results.

      
Why ten to twelve months?
Your brain rewires around what you repeat. In one well-known study, new daily habits took a median of 66 days to become automatic, and some took far longer (Lally et al., 2010). Ten months, or twelve for the 37X Autonomy Protocol, gives each new pattern enough repetition to hold under real pressure, and it is short enough to keep the vision urgent.

      
Do I need a clear vision before I apply?
No. Drawing it properly is Pivot 01. Most leaders arrive with a vision that is too safe. Bring whatever you have, and we'll raise it together.

      
What happens after I apply?
We read every application personally. If there is a fit, you are invited to a private Strategy Session where we draft your vision and locate your ceiling. If there isn't, we'll tell you why and point you somewhere better.
