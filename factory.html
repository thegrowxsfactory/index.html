Still weighing it up?

How is this different from executive coaching?

Coaching works on what feels urgent. GROW$X is a fixed sequence of ten pivots, inner and outer, on your live role, measured in your numbers.

What happens in the Strategy Session?

Where you settled, what it costs, and what one step past your horizon looks like. You leave with a plan either way.

Which programme is right for me?

Blueprint for value creators. Career Kaihagyo for reinvention and succession. Autonomy for legacy. No need to choose before the session.

Who leads the programmes?

Seasoned executives with proven cross-industry results. Not certified coaches.

Is the excavation work therapy?

No. It is performance work, tied to your vision and your numbers.

I am a senior professional, not yet in the top seat. Is this for me?

Yes. Career Kaihagyo is built for you, one-to-one or with peers, measured on scope, title, pay and the seat.

Can a leadership team or a family business take the programme together?

Yes. GROW$XTeam and Succession Planning are built for one organisation; NextGen for a family’s successors.

Do we need a clear vision before applying?

No. Drawing it is Pivot 01. Bring what you have.
