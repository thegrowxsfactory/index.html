Questions

Before you book.

How is this different from executive coaching?

Most coaching works on whatever feels urgent this week. GROW$X is a fixed sequence of ten pivots aimed at one vision, covering the inner work (mindset, identity, self-worth) and the outer work (network, brand, power, impact, status) on your live role, and measured on the numbers you are judged by.

What happens in the Strategy Session?

We go through where you have settled, what it is costing you and what one step past your current horizon looks like. We draft your vision together. If there is a fit, we recommend the programme and format. You leave with clarity either way.

Which programme is right for me?

Blueprint is the flagship for value creators. Career Kaihagyo is for career reinvention and succession. Autonomy is the 12-month elite engagement for family enterprise owners and successors. You do not need to choose before the session.

Who leads the programmes?

Seasoned executives with long cross-industry experience and proven track records of delivering results, who bring coaching skill and emotional and cross-cultural intelligence. We do not use certified coaches.

Is the excavation work therapy?

No. It is performance work. We go into mindset, identity and self-worth because that is where high performers’ ceilings usually sit, and we keep it tied to your vision and your results.

I am a senior professional, not yet in the top seat. Is this for me?

Yes. GROW$X Career Kaihagyo is built for career builders: radical career reinvention at the moments that matter, one-to-one or with a peer group. Results are measured on what your next employer or board looks at: scope, title, compensation and the seat itself.

Can a leadership team or a family business take the programme together?

Yes. GROW$XTeam and Succession Planning are built for one company or family business, with a scorecard for every member and a shared target. NextGen Bespoke is an in-house academy for a family’s successors. Open peer groups bring together non-competing leaders, or successors from different families.

Do we need a clear vision before applying?

No. Drawing it properly is Pivot 01. Most leaders, professionals, teams and families arrive with a vision that is too safe. Bring whatever you have.
