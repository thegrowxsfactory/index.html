The Gap

Your safe forecast is the problem.

Leaders deliver the forecast their conditioning allows. It looks like performance. It is a number agreed in advance, and it hides two losses nobody audits.

40%Zone 1The Below-Baseline Deficit

Value lost below your own safe forecast: the target watered down, the hard conversation avoided, the decision delayed. Whether you lead a company, a portfolio, a family enterprise, a board, a public mandate or your own career, it is the floor you never reached.

60%Zone 2The Above-Baseline Multiplier

The upside above the safe forecast: the multiple your seat could return, in enterprise value, portfolio returns, legacy, national impact or the next seat. It stays locked until the leader is grown, because no plan reaches past the person who drew it.
